How it works

  1. Choose an old and a new version of a policy, from the Policy Library, an official CMS link, or pasted text.

  3. Every quote is checked against the source text. Anything not found is clearly flagged.
  4. Confirm each finding in the side-by-side diff before you act on it.

What CareImpact AI does

CareImpact AI compares two versions of a Medicare coverage policy (LCD), finds the sections that changed with a deterministic diff, and asks a small language model to classify each change and draft operational implications.

Every evidence quote is checked against the supplied source text. Quotes that cannot be found are flagged as unsupported.

Responsible use

  • Generated analysis is not official CMS guidance or legal/compliance advice.
  • Synthetic samples are fictional and labelled as such; they never describe a real LCD.
  • Unverified version pairs are never presented as confirmed policy changes.
  • Confidence labels are qualitative and not calibrated probabilities.
  • No patient-identifiable data is used or stored.
  • CPT codes are subject to AMA license terms; the collector does not accept license terms on your behalf.

Frequently asked questions

How do I run my first comparison?

Click Try a sample comparison on the Home page, or open the Policy Library and press Compare on any row.

What does "verified" mean?

The CMS page's document ID, version and title matched what was requested and real policy content was extracted. Unverified pairs are never shown as confirmed changes.

Why does it say "baseline only"?

The local AI server (Ollama) is not running or the model is not installed, so the app uses the rule-based diff baseline. Install Ollama and run ollama pull qwen2.5:1.5b to enable model analysis.

What does "not found in source" mean?

The quote could not be matched to the policy text. The model may have paraphrased or invented it, so check that finding by hand.

Can I use my own policy text?

Yes. Use the Paste text tab. Put each heading on its own line so the text is split into sections.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Pasted text is processed in memory for the comparison and is not saved. The assistant's conversation lives only in your browser tab.
